Credit Cards

Credit cards are a form of borrowing. Understanding balances, minimum payments, APR, and repayment can help students make informed decisions.

💳 Credit Card Anatomy

A credit card lets you borrow up to a credit limit and repay what you owe under the card agreement. Important terms include balance, minimum payment, interest, APR, and credit limit.

Credit limit
Maximum amount available under the account.
Balance
Amount currently owed.
APR
Annual percentage rate for borrowing costs, subject to the agreement.
Minimum payment
The smallest payment required by the agreement for that billing cycle.
