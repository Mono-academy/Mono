Lesson 5.1 — Compound Growth

Explore how growth can build on previous growth over time—and why real investment returns are not guaranteed.

🌱 Compound Growth

Compound growth means growth can build on previous growth. In investing, actual returns vary and are not guaranteed.

TimeMore periods can give compounding more opportunities.
ContributionsNew money can add to the amount participating in future growth.
VariationReal investment returns can be positive or negative.
