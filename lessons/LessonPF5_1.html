Lesson 4.3 — Opportunity Cost

Learn to see the trade-offs behind money and time decisions.

⚖️ Opportunity Cost

Opportunity cost is the value of the next-best realistic alternative you give up when you choose something else. It makes trade-offs visible.

ChoiceWhat you decide.
AlternativeThe best realistic option not chosen.
Trade-offWhat changes because resources went elsewhere.

🔄 Trade-Off Cards

Save $100More progress toward a future goal.
Spend $100More value from an experience now.

There is no universal “correct” choice. The skill is identifying what the decision gives up.
