Lesson 5.2 — Account Types

Understand the difference between the account that holds money and the investments inside it.

🗂️ Account Types

The account is the container; the investments are the assets inside it. Account types can have different tax treatment, contribution rules, withdrawal rules, fees, and purposes.

Taxable brokerageFlexible investing with applicable tax rules.
Retirement accountDesigned for retirement with specific rules.
Education-focused accountMay have rules aimed at qualified education expenses.
