🧺 Asset Allocation

Asset allocation is how a portfolio is divided among asset categories such as stocks, bonds, and cash. A thoughtful mix considers goals, time horizon, and risk tolerance.

StocksGrowth potential with meaningful volatility and loss risk.
BondsDebt investments with interest-rate and credit risks.
CashLiquid money useful for near-term needs.
DiversificationCan reduce concentration risk but cannot eliminate losses.
