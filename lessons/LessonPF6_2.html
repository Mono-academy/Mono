Lesson 6.2 — Consumer Protection

Practice slowing down, verifying information, and protecting sensitive information.

🔐 Consumer Protection

Good consumer habits include verifying who is contacting you, slowing down under pressure, protecting sensitive information, and keeping records of important transactions.

VerifyUse known official channels.
PauseUrgency is a reason to slow down.
ProtectKeep passwords and verification codes private.
DocumentKeep receipts and confirmations.
