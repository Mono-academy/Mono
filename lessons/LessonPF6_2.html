Lesson 6.1 — Insurance Basics

Learn the language of insurance and how to read coverage before relying on it.

🛡️ Insurance Basics

Insurance is a contract that can provide financial protection against specified risks, subject to the policy's terms, limits, deductibles, premiums, and exclusions.

PremiumAmount paid for coverage.
DeductibleAmount the policyholder may pay before covered benefits apply, depending on the policy.
LimitMaximum amount the insurer may pay for a covered loss under the policy.
ExclusionA situation or loss the policy does not cover.

🔎 Policy Decoder

Before relying on coverage, locate the premium, deductible, limits, covered events, exclusions, and claim requirements. For a real policy, read the actual documents.

Think like a decoder: “What is covered? What is not? What would I pay? What is the maximum benefit?”
