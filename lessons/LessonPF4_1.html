Lesson 4.1 — Emergency Funds

Build a cash cushion for unexpected needs while keeping planned goals separate.

🚨 Emergency Funds

An emergency fund is money set aside for unexpected, necessary expenses or an interruption in income. It is different from money saved for a planned purchase.

EmergencyUnexpected essential repair or temporary income disruption.
Planned goalA known future expense such as a laptop or trip.
AccessibleEmergency money should generally be reasonably available when needed.
RebuildUsing the fund for a real emergency is part of the plan; rebuilding can come next.
