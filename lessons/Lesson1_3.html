Lesson 1.3: The T-Account System

[cite: 1]

[cite: 1] A T-account is a visual tool used in double-entry bookkeeping to track financial transactions. It has the account title at the top, debits on the left, and credits on the right. It looks like the letter T. Each transaction must have an equal amount of debits and credits in at least 2 accounts.[cite: 1]
